How to play Riichi Mahjong

A game for 4 people, a bit like rummy. For your first game you only need this side.

Getting started

Four people around the table.

1Mix the tiles face down and build a square wall together.
2Everyone takes 13 tiles and stands them in front of them.
3A die decides who starts (the dealer). Play goes counter-clockwise.
Mix · deal · play
3

Your turn

Draw one, discard one.

1You draw a tile from the wall.
2You decide which one you do not need.
3You discard it face up in front of you.

Then the player on your right goes. At the end of your turn you always hold 13 tiles.

Draw · decide · discard
4

Winning

The first to complete their hand wins.

4 sets + 1 pair, using the tile you drew or the one another player just threw.

Say it out loud —“Tsumo!” if you drew it, “Ron!” if another player threw it— and show your hand.

In your first game nothing else is needed: no calls, no points. When you want more, turn the sheet over.

Tsumo! · Ron!

Quick guide · side 2 of 2

Calls

If someone throws the tile you need, you may take it.

Chiisequence, only from your left
Pontriplet, from any player
Kanquad: draw an extra tile

Say it out loud and lay the set face up, with the taken tile sideways. Your hand becomes “open”.

Winning properly: the yaku

Besides a complete hand you need at least 1 yaku.

Tsumoyou draw it: all 3 pay
Ronanother throws it: they pay
RiichiOne tile from winning and no calls: discard one sideways and add a 1,000 stick.
TanyaoOnly tiles from 2 to 8
YakuhaiTriplet of dragons or of your wind

Furiten: if you already threw a tile you would win on, you cannot win by ron.

When someone wins, the payer leaves the sticks on the table and the winner takes them. The value comes from han (yaku and dora) and fu (mini points).

Rounds and the end of the game

You play hand after hand, and whoever ends with the most points wins.

東East round1234
南South round1234

If the dealer wins they stay dealer. If not, the compass turns: everyone takes the next wind and the dealer passes to the right.

Tonpuusen: East round only (short). Hanchan: East and South (standard).

Dora and the dead wall

Bonus tiles and tiles set aside.

DORA +1 han

When the wall is opened an indicator is turned face up: the next tile after it is the dora, and each one in your hand adds 1 han. Red fives do too.

Dead wall: 14 tiles set aside (7 stacks), out of play unless someone makes a kan.

Handy words

To follow what people say at the table.

Tenpai — you are one tile from winning. Noten: you are not.
Dealer — the one who plays first (East seat).
Seat wind / round wind — yours / the current round’s. Yours changes as hands pass.
Pond — your discards, face up in front of you.
Han · fu — the two measures used to count a hand.
